# Photo album shortcuts

Personal photo viewers show baby and wedding album toggles for the profile owner, on phones, desktop and the installed app. The wedding button adds the current photo to that person's wedding album without navigating or closing the viewer; after saving it turns green with a checkmark. A second tap removes the membership. The same behavior applies when viewing a shared photo in a personal album context. People without a wedding album retain a disabled icon. The baby toggle adds the current photo to that person's nursery album, and a second tap removes that membership. Shared Family photos and gallery viewers do not show these personal album shortcuts. Compact viewers keep every control, including the baby icon, visible on one row by letting the buttons narrow to the available width. The shared photo viewer also shows a Family photos toggle, including when opened from a gallery, a personal album or a shared link. Green means the current photo belongs to the selected album. Selecting the same icon again removes that membership immediately; a later tap adds it again. People without an eligible nursery album retain a disabled shortcut with an explanatory accessible label.

`POST /api/media/<id>/collections` accepts `{collection, scope, included}`. `included` is an optional boolean and defaults to true for existing clients. Family photos (`photos`), nursery (`nursery:<person>`) and wedding (`wedding:<unit>`) support both inclusion and removal. The server verifies nursery/wedding changes originate from the person's owned or tagged album, and that a wedding belongs to that person. Existing write-token, origin and transaction protections apply. Nonphotos and invalid membership values are rejected. Explicit responses for each album override stale photo cards or out-of-order responses from another album.

The `media_collections` table holds unique references to the original media ID. Native categories are also reported as membership, without a migration over existing photos. Removing a native Family, nursery or wedding photo from its original album moves its category to the gallery while preserving its ID, file, tags, profile references, versions and other memberships. Removing a linked photo removes only the selected album reference. Both affected album revisions are invalidated. Media files, captions, tags and face labels are shared. Lists and nursery summaries combine native and linked photos without duplicates. Soft deletion hides the photo everywhere; undo restores all memberships. Export includes `mediaCollections`.

Pending saves are keyed by photo and album. Repeated taps cannot duplicate a save; failure preserves the previous selection for retry. Out-of-order successful adds are reconciled, while unrelated album responses and stale cards cannot restore a removed Family selection. A response for a previous photo cannot mark the next one. Desktop shows the Family label alongside the icon; phones retain a compact icon with an accessible add/remove label.
