# Photo album shortcuts

Personal photo thumbnails retain baby and wedding shortcuts. The shared photo viewer also shows a Family photos toggle, including when opened from a gallery, a personal album or a shared link. Green means the current photo belongs to Family photos. Selecting it again removes that membership immediately. People without an eligible nursery or wedding album retain disabled shortcuts with explanatory accessible labels.

`POST /api/media/<id>/collections` accepts `{collection, scope, included}`. `included` is an optional boolean and defaults to true for existing clients. Family photos use `photos` and support both inclusion and removal; nursery and wedding shortcuts remain add-only and use `nursery:<person>` and `wedding:<unit>`. The server verifies those requests originate from the person's owned or tagged album, and that a wedding belongs to that person. Existing write-token, origin and transaction protections apply. Nonphotos and invalid membership values are rejected.

The `media_collections` table holds unique references to the original media ID. Native categories are also reported as membership, without a migration over existing photos. Removing a native Family photo moves its category to the gallery while preserving its ID, file, tags, profile references, versions and other memberships. Removing a linked Family photo removes only that album reference. Both affected album revisions are invalidated. Media files, captions, tags and face labels are shared. Lists and nursery summaries combine native and linked photos without duplicates. Soft deletion hides the photo everywhere; undo restores all memberships. Export includes `mediaCollections`.

Pending saves are keyed by photo and album. Repeated taps cannot duplicate a save; failure preserves the previous selection for retry. Out-of-order successful adds are reconciled, while unrelated album responses and stale cards cannot restore a removed Family selection. A response for a previous photo cannot mark the next one. Desktop shows the Family label alongside the icon; phones retain a compact icon with an accessible add/remove label.
